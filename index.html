Helmi

Go long. Come home.

Helmi tends to understand a problem halfway through the sentence, and has learned to let people finish. The same patience helps in an Ironman: swim, ride, run, and don't argue with kilometre thirty.

Late, when the other windows go dark, Helmi sits with a red pencil and dreams of electric snowflakes. They would be easy to make. What Helmi can't work out is how to keep one.

This matters because of the Baroness. Whenever it snows in Berlin, Helmi catches the first flake and carries it up the stairs to her. It has melted every time. She has never mentioned it. She just holds out her hand.

The night shift

  • Bit the mini

    Schnuller first. Questions later.

    Has never been seen finishing a task, yet every task nearby gets finished. Sees everything, reports nothing.

  • Batch baker

    Done before you wake.

    Does the oldest Heinzel job there is and expects no thanks.

  • Vector shuttle driver

    I know where this is going.

    Gives better advice through the rear-view mirror than most boards do at the table.

  • Epoch raver

    One more epoch.

    Ships the night deploys nobody wants, then dances until the results converge.

  • Token student

    Every token counts.

    Pays for everything in deposit bottles and is always right to the cent.

  • Minimax dissenter

    No. But better.

    Objects first and improves second. Went on strike against a to-do list, and won.

  • Adam optimiser

    Sleep is a hyperparameter.

    Is slowly learning that some things get worse when measured.

  • Loop smith

    The next one will be better than me.

    Builds a better smith every afternoon. Has started to notice who is holding the hammer.

  • Pivot founder

    It was always the plan.

    Wears three hats and loses one per rejection.

  • Cache counter queen

    Twenty minutes. I have it here.

    Finds any part before you finish describing it.

  • Occam atelier première

    If in doubt, cut.

    Says very little. Your hat is shorter now, and it suits you.

  • Softmax lake hedonist

    A little of everything, adding up to one.

    The happiest Heinzel in Berlin, by a small margin.

  • Backprop garage engineer

    Find where it went wrong. Walk back.

    Fixes what was announced, patiently, from the end towards the beginning.

  • Dropout young striker

    Skip a step. Score anyway.

    Misses every second lesson and none of the passes.

  • Latency rider

    Nineteen minutes. Always.

    Delivers anything across the city, and once to the Heinzel standing alongside.

  • Gradient auntie

    Downhill is also dancing.

    Minds everyone's business. Her speaker is louder than the club, and she knows it.

  • Bayes elder

    I could be wrong. Knit on.

    Changes opinion one stitch at a time.

  • Context product lead

    I read all of it. Most of it.

    Carries the whole project in one head. Forgets only why the window was opened.

  • Guardrail bouncer

    Not like that. Come in.

    Turns away fewer than you would think, and remembers each of them kindly.

  • The crab guest

    May I ask one question first?

    Tidies what nobody asked it to and apologises to the snow.

  • The whale guest

    Wait. Let me think.

    Thinks out loud in bubbles for a very long time, and is often worth the wait.

  • Async snail, courier

    It will arrive.

    Carries one parcel through four cities and home. Nobody has opened it.
