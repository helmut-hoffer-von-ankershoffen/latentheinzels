Privacy notice

The German version of this page prevails.

Status: 22 November 2026.

In short

  • Without your consent this website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It uses no advertising services. It keeps one entry for the film player in session storage and one for your Analytics choice in local storage (section 7). Its fonts, images and video come from your device or from this website.
  • Google Analytics loads only if you allow it. Google then receives data about your visit, including your IP address, and sets cookies. You can withdraw at any time at the start of section 5a; every page links here as Privacy in its footer.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Both process technical data such as your IP address (sections 3 and 4). Cloudflare also asks your browser to report failed connections to it.
  • The film of the top song, with its music, comes from this website. It starts loading and playing when you open the main page, unless your device asks for reduced motion or data saving (section 2).
  • SoundCloud's players load only when you press play. SoundCloud then receives your IP address and may set cookies (section 5).

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR is the person named in the imprint, at the postal address given there.

Email: helmut@heinzel.team. Use the same address for any data protection question.

2. What we process ourselves

The website consists of static files. We run no server of our own and keep no access logs of our own. We analyse visits only with Google Analytics and only if you allow it (section 5a). Otherwise the only data we receive ourselves is what you write to us (section 6).

The film of the top song, "Berlin Evening: Singularity (Extended Version)", is a video with sound that this website hosts itself. When you open a page that shows it, your browser starts loading and playing it, muted if it doesn't allow sound without a press: your first click or tap on the page then switches the sound on. With reduced motion or data saving switched on, it loads only when you press play, and after you paused it, only when you press play again, reload or come to the website anew. Its requests reach GitHub and Cloudflare like those for the website's other files (sections 3 and 4). No video or music service is involved.

The film comes in two sizes, about 45 MB and 93 MB for the whole film. To pick one, the player looks at your screen's size and, where your browser reports it, the speed of your connection. Both are read on your device, and the player neither stores nor sends them. If you allow Google Analytics, that receives the screen size by its own measurement (section 5a).

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

The website's files are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., USA (part of Microsoft), and reach you through Cloudflare (section 4). According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when a page is visited, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. Cloudflare can pass your IP address on to GitHub with the request. GitHub's privacy statement says further technical details may be collected, such as the time of the request, the page and the referrer. We have no influence on these logs.

  • Purpose: secure and stable delivery of the website.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in publishing the website).
  • Recipient: GitHub Inc., as processor or as an independent controller for its own security logs.
  • Third country: USA. GitHub states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795, Art. 45 GDPR) and otherwise relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Retention: determined by GitHub.

4. Delivery, protection and error reports: Cloudflare

This website's domain uses the name servers of Cloudflare, Inc., USA, and every request to the website passes through Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare decrypts the connection, processes your IP address, the address requested, the time and technical details your browser sends, keeps copies of files to deliver them faster, and may log requests to operate and protect its network.

Cloudflare's responses also ask browsers that support Network Error Logging to remember for up to seven days where to report failed connections. If a connection to this website fails, such a browser may send a report to a.nel.cloudflare.com with technical details such as the address, the kind of error and your IP address. Successful visits are not reported this way.

  • Purpose: reachability, secure and fast delivery, detection of network errors.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Third country: USA. Cloudflare states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and relies on standard contractual clauses as a fallback.
  • Retention: determined by Cloudflare.

5. Music players: SoundCloud, only after you press play

Opening this website sends nothing to SoundCloud. Only when you press a play button does your browser load SoundCloud's player from w.soundcloud.com and from the servers that player uses. SoundCloud (SoundCloud Global Limited & Co. KG, Germany, and its affiliates) then receives your IP address, technical details of your browser, this website's address and what you play. It may set cookies or use similar storage, may link this to your SoundCloud account if you are signed in, and may transfer data to other countries, including the USA. Its own privacy policy applies.

  • Purpose: playing the music you chose.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by pressing play after the notice beside the players (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, for storage on your device, § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Withdrawal: reload the page and don't press play again. You can delete what SoundCloud stored in your browser's settings.
  • Retention: determined by SoundCloud.

5a. Visit statistics: Google Analytics, only if you allow it

Your choice: Google Analytics is allowed.Your choice: Google Analytics is declined.You haven't chosen yet.

Until you press Accept, no script from Google loads and nothing is sent to Google. The same holds after Decline. If you accept, your browser loads Google's measurement script (gtag.js) from googletagmanager.com and sends data about your visit to Google's Analytics servers (google-analytics.com and analytics.google.com). We use Google Analytics 4 with the measurement ID G-EL18RTQ5GY, without a Tag Manager container, advertising features or Google Signals.

  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which processes the data for us under Google's data processing terms, which we have accepted.
  • Purpose: to count visits and learn which pages, languages and countries they come from and how the website is used.
  • Data: a pseudonymous client and session identifier, the pages you open with their address and title, the page you came from, language, date and time, browser, operating system and device details including screen size, and coarse location down to the city. Collection of granular location and device data is on, as Google sets it by default. Enhanced measurement is on with all seven of its measurements: page views, scrolls, clicks on links to other sites, site search, video engagement, file downloads and form interactions. Google says that it uses the IP addresses of visitors in the EU, Switzerland and the UK to derive the coarse location and then discards them, without logging or storing them, and that it receives their data on servers there before forwarding it for processing.
  • Settings: all four options for sharing data with Google, Google Signals, collection of user-provided data and personalisation of ads (in all 307 regions) are switched off. Redaction of email addresses in event data is on. Redaction of query parameters in page addresses is off.
  • Cookies: _ga tells browsers apart by a random identifier, _ga_EL18RTQ5GY keeps the state of the session. Both are set for this website's host, by default for up to 2 years from when they were last renewed, and are sent with requests to this website.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, for storing and reading on your device, § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Third country: Google may process the data in the USA, also through Google LLC. Google states that Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) and otherwise relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Retention: event and user data for 2 months, and new activity does not restart that period. Aggregated standard reports are not limited by this setting and can be kept longer.
  • Withdrawal: press “Withdraw consent” at the start of this section. Measurement stops, both cookies are deleted and the page reloads without Google's script. This doesn't affect what was processed before.

We also use Google Search Console to see how the website appears in Google's search. It adds no script, cookie or tracking of visitors to this website and shows us aggregated statistics from Google's own search.

6. Contact by email: Cloudflare Email Routing and Resend

Incoming email to the address above is received by Cloudflare (Email Routing) and forwarded to a mailbox of the controller. Cloudflare processes sender and recipient addresses, metadata and the message content as far as needed for forwarding. Please do not send sensitive data by ordinary email.

Outgoing email from that domain is sent through the Resend service (Resend, USA). Resend processes recipient address, metadata and message content. In its data processing agreement Resend states that it applies standard contractual clauses and refers to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

  • Purpose: answering your enquiry and communicating with you.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry aims at a contract or pre-contractual measures, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).
  • Retention: we delete your message once your enquiry is dealt with and no legal retention duty applies. Cloudflare and Resend apply their own periods.

7. Cookies and browser storage (§ 25 TDDDG)

Without your consent the website sets no cookies. The cookies of Google Analytics are described in section 5a. In local storage the website keeps one entry, latentheinzels-analytics, with the value allow or decline, so that it can respect your choice (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The entry is never sent and stays until you change it or delete this website's data in your browser.

In session storage the film player keeps one entry, latentheinzels-film, with five values: film (the film's file name), time (how far it has played), paused (whether you paused it), muted (whether you muted it) and heard (whether it has played with sound). The entry serves only to respect your pause and mute and to let the film carry on when you change pages. It is never sent to us or anyone else, applies to that browser tab only, and your browser deletes it when the tab's session ends.

Your browser may cache the website's files as usual. The error-reporting instruction from Cloudflare is described in section 4, what SoundCloud may store after a press in section 5.

8. Links to other websites

The website links to the band's profiles on music, video and social platforms (the Channels row), to the top song's film on YouTube and, in the imprint, to the controller's own website. These are plain links: nothing is loaded from those sites before you follow one. Only then do you leave this website, and the operator of the target site processes your data under its own privacy notice.
